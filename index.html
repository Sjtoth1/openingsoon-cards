Questions
Where does the data come from?

Public records (state liquor applications, county health permits, city building permits), published local press, venue websites and public business listings. Business data only. Every row links to its source.

How early is it?

Liquor applications land months before opening; permits and pre-opening inspections weeks before; press usually last. The same system has posted openings on Instagram two weeks ahead of any coverage.

Do you include chains?

Yes. The public feed skips chain units; the sheet keeps them and says why they were skipped, because a new unit is still a new account for you.

Owner contact details?

No. We publish the business, its address, website and social handle, and the licensee name when it is on the public filing. We do not scrape personal emails or phone numbers.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from the link in your receipt. Access runs to the end of the paid month.

Whoever calls first gets the account.

Operators pick their distributor, POS and insurer during the build-out, months before opening day. This is the list of who to call this week.
