Questions

Where does the data come from?

Public records (state liquor-licence applications, county health permits, city building permits), published local press, venue websites and public business listings. Business data only. Every row links to its source.

How early is it?

Liquor applications land months before opening; permits and pre-opening inspections weeks before; press usually last. The same system has posted openings on Instagram two weeks ahead of any press coverage.

Do you include chains?

Yes. The public feed skips chain units; the sheet keeps them and says why they were skipped, because a new unit is still a new account for you.

Can I get other metros?

Any US metro with public liquor and permit data can be added in about two weeks. Ask.

Owner contact details?

No. We publish the business, its address, website and social handle, and the licensee name when it is on the public filing. We do not scrape personal emails or phone numbers.
